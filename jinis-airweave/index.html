03

Growing up, one night at a time.

Children arrive at JINIS at six and leave at twelve. In between, they grow taller, stronger and more independent, often faster than anyone expects.

airweave grows with them. Its mattresses are made in sections, and each zone (shoulders, hips, legs) can be adjusted softer or firmer. As a child’s body changes, the bed can be re-fitted to the child who sleeps in it.

In many Japanese homes, a child’s height is marked on a wooden pillar, year after year. At JINIS, six of those years are spent here.

Marks on the pillar · age 6 to 12

04

Made for everyday boarding life.

A residence shared by many children asks a lot of a bed: cleanliness, durability, and care that fits into a daily routine. The answer sits inside — airfiber®, a woven structure of resin fibre that is mostly air.
